Receipt photos are not stored. A photo is reduced in size on your phone, sent once to be read, and then discarded. It is not saved in our database and not kept on our servers.

We do not collect: your name, phone number, home address, location, contacts, card numbers (not even the last digits), loyalty card numbers, the shop's address or the time of purchase. We instruct the reading service to leave such details out. Paragon has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking cookies and does not sell or share your data. Fonts and code are served from our own site, not from third parties.

3. Receipts can say more than you think

A list of purchases can indirectly reveal sensitive things: medicines or pregnancy tests (health), alcohol, halal or kosher products (religion, habits), contraceptives and similar. We do not analyse your receipts for such information, do not build profiles from it and never share it. It is only used to show you your own spending.

Want more privacy?
  • Don't scan receipts with purchases you consider private, for example from a pharmacy, or certain cosmetics or alcohol.
  • Add them by hand instead, with a neutral name such as "personal care" or "other", so your totals stay correct.
  • After a scan you can delete or rename any line before saving, and edit or delete any receipt later.

4. Legal basis

  • Performance of the agreement with you (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR / AVG): your account, storing your receipts and calculating your overview.
  • Legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)): the scan limit, technical logs and keeping the service secure.

6. How long we keep data

  • Until you delete it. Settings → "Delete my account and all data" removes your account, receipts, settings and scan counters immediately.
  • Accounts not used for 12 months are deleted automatically, with all their data. "Used" means opening the app while signed in. Export your data first if you want to keep it.
  • Sign-ups whose email address was never confirmed are deleted after 7 days.
  • Backups made by our database provider may contain deleted data for up to [7 days, check your Supabase plan], after which it is overwritten.

7. Security

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS) and data is encrypted at rest.
  • The database itself makes sure every user can reach only their own data.
  • Secret keys exist only on the server, never in the app.
  • Passwords are stored hashed. Choose a password you don't use elsewhere.

8. Automated processing

An AI model reads the text on your receipt and suggests a product name and category. You can check and correct every line. It makes no decisions about you and has no legal or similar effect on you.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and receive your data (Settings → "Export my data" gives you everything as a CSV file), to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw any consent. Write to [privacy@your-domain]; we reply within one month. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

10. Age

Paragon is intended for people aged 16 and over.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is used, we tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
